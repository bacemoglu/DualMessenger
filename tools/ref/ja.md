## How these map to site.json

- hero.title_1 and hero.title_2 = the first screenshot headline, split at its `<br>` (title_1 keeps its trailing punctuation, if any).
- hero.lede = the first screenshot subline.
- features.items[0].title = second headline, features.items[1].title = third headline, features.items[2].title = fourth headline (write each as one line, without `<br>`).
- The first sentences of features.items[0..2].text correspond to the matching sublines; keep their wording where the English matches.
- features.also[3].text (iPad multitasking) = the Split View subline, but write the app name as "Dual Messenger+" (with the plus).

- Use your WhatsApp account on other devices, including iPad and iPhone Duo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Dual Messenger+ app for iPhone and iPad, and when you visit this website.

In short

  • There is no account to create. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your WhatsApp messages travel directly between your device and WhatsApp. We cannot see them.
  • Notes, reminders, recordings and AI bot conversations are stored on your device.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Subscriptions are sold by Apple. A service called RevenueCat checks whether your subscription is active, using an anonymous ID.

1. Who we are

Dual Messenger+ is an independent app. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer of Dual Messenger+. You can reach us at info@dualmessengerplus.com.

Dual Messenger+ is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

2. What stays on your device

Most of what the app handles never leaves your iPhone or iPad.

WhatsApp accounts. The app opens WhatsApp’s own web service inside the app. When you link an account, the login session is stored on your device so that you stay signed in. Your messages, contacts, photos and files are exchanged directly between your device and WhatsApp. They do not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot read, store or share them. WhatsApp handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Notes and reminders. These are saved in the app’s storage on your device. Reminder notifications are scheduled by the device itself.

Voice recordings and transcriptions. Audio that you record or import, and the text created from it, are saved on your device.

AI chat bot. The bot uses a language model that is stored on your device. Your questions and its answers are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

App lock. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives your face or fingerprint data. If you set a passcode in the app, it is stored on your device.

Location. If you use the location feature, the app reads your current location to show it on a map and to create a link that you choose to share. We do not receive or store your location.

You can delete notes, reminders and recordings in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes its data from your device. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, this data may be part of your backup, which is controlled by you and Apple.

3. Permissions the app asks for

The app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it:

  • Camera: to take photos and videos that you send in your chats.
  • Photos: to save photos and videos from your chats to your library.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages and audio for Voice To Text.
  • Speech Recognition: to turn audio into text.
  • Face ID: to unlock the app.
  • Location: to show your position and create the link you share.
  • Notifications: to remind you at the time you set.

You can refuse any of these, or turn them off later in the iOS Settings app. The features that depend on them will then stop working.

4. Services from other companies

Apple: purchases. Subscriptions are bought with your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment, so we never receive your card or billing details. Apple gives us sales statistics that do not identify you.

Apple: speech recognition. Voice To Text uses the speech recognition built into iOS. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on your device or on its servers. We do not receive the audio or the text. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

RevenueCat: subscription status. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app, the details of your purchases in the app (such as the plan and the purchase date) and basic technical information (such as the app version). It does not receive your name, your email address, your messages or anything you create in the app. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf, may do so in the United States, and describes its practices in its privacy policy.

WhatsApp. Your use of WhatsApp inside the app is covered by WhatsApp’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider, which processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. We do not use this data to identify you.

6. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use this only to answer you, and we keep it only for as long as we need it to handle your request.

7. Children

Dual Messenger+ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Because the app does not collect personal data, there is usually nothing for us to hand over, correct or delete. You control what is stored on your device.

Depending on where you live, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Türkiye, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, write to info@dualmessengerplus.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our contract with you to provide the app and your subscription, and on our legitimate interest in answering your messages.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to info@dualmessengerplus.com.
